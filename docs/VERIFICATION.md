## Known financial boundaries

Tracked inventory costs use weighted consumption snapshots; other service components remain estimates. Salary profiles are reference values and do not post payroll automatically. Expense revisions preserve history and closed months remain immutable; reopening is not implemented. Operating profit is separate from net profit and recorded cash flow. Tax expenses are entered rather than calculated; cash reconciliation is an owner declaration. Forecast and payback projections depend on explicit assumptions. Cancellation is limited to unpaid invoices without stock consumption or linked completed jobs; refunds and stock returns remain unavailable. Completed but uninvoiced jobs do not yet contribute revenue or P&L costs.
